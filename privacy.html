Privacy Policy

Last updated September 1, 2026

Fleet Ready Logistics is an Amazon Delivery Service Partner based in Lodi, New Jersey. This policy explains what personal information we collect from people who apply for a job with us, why we collect it, how we handle it, and how you can opt out — including the mobile phone numbers applicants give us on our employment application.

Information we collect

  • Name — your first and last name.
  • Mobile phone number — the number you enter on our employment application.
  • Email address.
  • Application details — the answers you give on the application, such as work eligibility, driving experience and availability, and a resume if you choose to upload one.

We collect this information directly from you, when you fill out our application. We do not buy contact lists and we do not obtain applicant phone numbers from data brokers.

Why we collect it

  • To review your application and decide whether to invite you to an interview.
  • To contact you about scheduling, confirming, or rescheduling an interview.
  • To tell you the status of your application.
  • To complete hiring and onboarding paperwork if you are hired.

We use applicant information for our own hiring only. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Text messages and consent

Our employment application asks for your mobile number and shows an optional consent checkbox. Ticking it is how you opt in to text messages, and we store the exact wording you agreed to along with the date. The wording on the form is:

“Text me about my application. Fleet Ready Logistics may send text messages about interviews, scheduling and onboarding to the mobile number above. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of employment or of applying.”

Consent is not a condition of employment or of applying. Every message we send identifies Fleet Ready Logistics and tells you how to stop. Full details of the program are on our SMS Terms & Conditions page.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any text message we send you and we will stop texting that number. You can also email hr@fleetreadylogistics.com and ask us to stop texting you, or ask us to delete your contact information. Opting out of text messages does not withdraw your application — we will contact you by phone or email instead.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for anyone else’s marketing. We share it only where it is needed to run our hiring process:

  • With the service providers that operate our messaging, hiring and payroll systems on our behalf, and only so they can provide that service to us.
  • Where we are required to by law, legal process, or a lawful government request.

As stated above, mobile numbers and text-messaging consent are excluded from any sharing for marketing or promotional purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep applicant information for as long as we need it to run the hiring process and to meet the record-keeping obligations that apply to employers. If you are hired, your information becomes part of your employment record and is kept for the length of your employment and the period required afterward. If you would like your information deleted sooner, email hr@fleetreadylogistics.com and we will delete it unless we are required to keep it.

Security

Applicant information is held on access-controlled systems, and access is limited to the Fleet Ready Logistics staff who run hiring and onboarding.
