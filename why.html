How Training Works

We hire in training classes so everyone starts together. Here's what the process looks like:

1️⃣

Apply + Interview

Quick online application, then a short phone call with our team. We're looking for reliable people, not perfect resumes.

2️⃣

3-Day Paid Training

Mandatory safety and delivery training. You're on the clock from day one. Amazon provides the curriculum, we provide the support.

3️⃣

Nursery Routes

Your first routes are lighter so you can learn the area and the process. You ramp up gradually over your first couple weeks.

4️⃣

Full Routes

Once you're comfortable, you run full routes across local neighborhoods. Most drivers find their rhythm within the first month.
