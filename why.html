The Independence Factor

One of the things drivers tell us they love most: once you're on your route, it's your time. You set your pace, you take your breaks when you want, you put your music or podcast on. No manager hovering. No customers arguing about coupons. Just you, the van, and the road.

You have to execute the deliveries — that's the job. But how you get through your route is up to you. For a lot of people coming from retail or food service, that freedom is life-changing.

Once you're comfortable, you run full routes across local neighborhoods. Most drivers find their rhythm within the first month.
